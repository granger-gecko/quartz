# About
My family history research is a case study in how to apply AI as a co-intelligence and co-creator. I want to tell the stories that never made it into the history books.

My own research covers four centuries of family history across Acadian, Loyalist, Québécois, Scottish, English and French lines. 

What I learn here shapes how I think about AI in the GLAM world (galleries, libraries, archives, and museums) and the B2B and B2B2G products being built around it.

This site is where that research turns into writing — a working notebook, updated as I learn.
