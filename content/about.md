# About

I'm Christopher Young — an executive advisor, board director, and researcher working at the intersection of AI, history, and governance.

My professional work spans product transformation, AI strategy, and board governance. My research work spans four centuries of family history across Acadian, Loyalist, Québécois, and Scottish lines — and increasingly, what AI can do with the kind of archival evidence that has always been too voluminous to read in a single lifetime.

This garden is where those two threads converge. It is not a polished publication. It is a working notebook, updated as I learn.
